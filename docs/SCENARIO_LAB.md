# RetailPulse Scenario Lab

## Purpose

The Scenario Lab is an interactive planning aid for exploring how an assumed improvement in 90-day second-purchase conversion could translate into additional orders and gross revenue. It is not a forecast, a churn model, a causal estimate or a profitability model.

## Historical baseline

The calculation uses three values generated by the reproducible Phase 3 pipeline:

- **Eligible customers:** identified first-time customers whose first invoice occurred at least 90 days before the end of the historical observation window.
- **Observed baseline repeat rate:** the share of that eligible group that placed a second distinct invoice within 90 days.
- **Identified-customer average order value:** the mean invoice revenue across identified-customer orders in the historical source period.

The Phase 3 summary publishes the identified-customer order count, mean order value and median order value. The Scenario Lab uses the mean as its revenue anchor and exposes a user-adjustable order-value percentage change.

## Calculation

For an eligible base \(N\), observed repeat rate \(r\), assumed absolute uplift \(u\) in percentage points, and scenario average order value \(A\):

\[
\text{Expected additional repeat customers} = N \times \frac{u}{100}
\]

\[
\text{Expected additional second orders} = \text{Expected additional repeat customers}
\]

\[
\text{Illustrative incremental gross revenue} =
\text{Expected additional second orders} \times A
\]

The scenario repeat rate is \(r+u\). Uplifts are bounded so the resulting repeat rate cannot exceed 100%.

The dashboard derives three assumption sets from the selected base-case uplift:

- **Conservative:** half the base-case uplift.
- **Base:** the selected uplift.
- **Stretch:** 1.5 times the selected uplift, capped at the remaining mathematical headroom below 100%.

The average order value adjustment is applied equally to each scenario. A sensitivity curve shows the revenue arithmetic from zero uplift through the stretch case.

## Interpretation rules

1. Uplifts are user-entered assumptions. They are not model outputs, industry benchmarks or observed treatment effects.
2. Fractional customers and orders represent expected values in a scenario calculation; they are not actual counts of people or transactions.
3. Each incremental repeat customer is assumed to place exactly one additional second order at the adjusted historical average order value.
4. Gross revenue is not profit or contribution. The dataset does not provide margin, campaign cost, acquisition cost, fulfilment capacity or a complete returns-adjusted accounting measure.
5. The source period ends on 9 December 2011. This is historical evidence, not a live retailer feed or a prediction about current customers.
6. A controlled experiment is required to estimate causal incremental impact before a retailer commits budget or operational capacity.

## Empty-state behaviour

The Scenario Lab is unavailable when there is no positive 90-day eligible base, no valid observed repeat rate, or no positive identified-customer average order value. Small synthetic fixtures may not contain enough observation time to create a complete 90-day cohort; this is a valid empty state. Re-run the core and Phase 3 pipelines after changing or downloading the source data.
